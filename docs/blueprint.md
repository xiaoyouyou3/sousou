# **App Name**: TuneFlow

## Core Features:

- Mood & Genre Input: Users select their current mood and desired music genre through a UI form.
- AI-Powered Score Generation: Leverage AI as a tool to create sheet music with Tone.js based on the mood and genre input.
- Music Playback: Play back the generated music based on the sheet music generated by AI.
- Score Visualization: Display the generated sheet music visually.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A vibrant purple (#A020F0) for creativity and imagination.
- Background color: Light lavender (#E6E6FA), a desaturated tint of purple, creates a soft backdrop that doesn't distract from the score.
- Accent color: Deep blue (#00008B) to highlight interactive elements.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter' sans-serif font for a clean, modern aesthetic. 
- Use simple, elegant icons representing different moods and genres.
- A clean, intuitive layout with the mood/genre selector at the top and sheet music/playback controls below.
- Subtle animations to visualize the music playback and sheet music elements.
